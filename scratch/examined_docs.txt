in food contact applications.  Superior ligand chemistry provides the excellent melt and SSP activity 
required for excellent polymer color and aesthetics.   
Approved for Food Contact
Tyzor® ACtivate™ catalysts complies with the requirements of US CFR177.1630 and 
EU Regulation 10/2011 when used in the manufacture of plastic for food contact materials.  
Tyzor® ACtivate™ 422 benefits can be fine-tuned in a variety of ways to fit each manufacturer’s unique

performance in demanding bottle designs.  
Application Benefits

DESCRIPTION Tyzor ® ACtivate™ 422 is a hydrolytically stable titanate catalyst specifically designed for PET bottle resin 
production.  Superior ligand chemistry provides excellent catalytic activity, producing PET resin and 
bottles with superior color and aesthetics.  
Tyzor® ACtivate™ 422 benefits can be fine-tuned in a variety of ways to fit each manufacturer’s unique 
requirements for a balance of manufacturing efficiency, product quality and performance improvements 
in demanding bottle applications.  
Application Benefits:
 Improved Process Efficiency and Throughput 
 Enhanced Resin and Bottle Properties  
 Improved Resin and Bottle Performance  
Tyzor® ACtivate™ 422 is a very effective esterification catalyst that can be used to increase 
the esterification reaction by up to 20%, useful for de-bottlenecking a process.  Alternatively the 
esterification temperature can be lowered to reduce diethylene glycol concentration in the resin.  
Tyzor® ACtivate™ 422 is a very effective melt polymerization catalyst that can be used to increase 
the melt plant rate by up to 15% or to decrease the temperature of the melt polymerization process, 
resulting in improved product quality.  Tyzor® ACtivate™ 422 has been shown to produce bottle resin 
and bottles with a reduced level of oligomers, acetaldehyde and haze.  
Tyzor® ACtivate™ 422 changes the crystallinity properties of the resin compared to an equivalent 
Sb catalyzed resin showing a higher onset of crystallation on heating and a lower onset of crystallation 
on cooling.  These changes can be exploited by a reduction in IPA content of up to 40%, producing 
significant financial benefits.  
Tyzor® ACtivate™ 422 is hydrolytically stable, which provides exceptional manufacturing versatility.  
It can beadded before or during esterification, to either the paste tank or the esterification reactors.  
Alternatively it can be added to the oligomer line after esterification.

APPLICATIONS P OLYUREA COATINGS SYSTEMS
Clearlink® 1000 is a liquid aliphatic chain extender for light-stable or color-critical aliphatic polyurea spray

APPLICATIONS C AST ELASTOMERS
Unilink® 1030 is designed to provide delayed “snap cures” in polyester polyol elastomers, allowing molds 
to be filled while the mixture remains relatively low in viscosity.  When gelling begins, it takes place rapidly, 
allowing the casting to be removed from the mold quickly for maximum productivity.  
Unilink® 1030 is a proprietary zirconium chelate in a reactive diol solvent.  It features a sharp viscosity 
increase profile that is very similar to mercury, but without the exposure risks or environmental hazards.  
Because the diol solvent reacts into the polyurethane it works without leaving any residue.  In addition, 
Unilink® 1030 is selective towards the gelling reaction, so it exhibits very low “blow” or water reactivity, and 
its low color makes it particularly suitable for color-critical formulations. 
Elastomers cured with Unilink® 1030 have excellent physical properties that are comparable, and in some 
cases superior to their mercury-cured equivalents.

APPLICATIONS S LABSTOCK FOAM
Unilink® 4200 improves tensile, tear and load-bearing properties in standard TDI foams and 
high-resilience foam systems, often at lower foam densities and with little effect on reaction kinetics 
or foam rise.  Foams made with Unilink® 4200 have a finer cell structure with less elongation in the 
direction of rise, which significantly improves compressive strength and dimensional stability.  
In polyester foams, Unilink® 4200 improves tear strength and load-bearing performance without 
degrading other foam properties.
COLD MOLDED FOAM
Unilink® 4200 significantly softens cold molded foams and reduces foam density.  Finished foams 
have higher tensile strength, improved tear and elongation as well as better mold flow, and can often 
be removed from the mold sooner.
