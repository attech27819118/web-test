DESCRIPTION
Dorf Ketal Tyzor® organic titanates and zirconates are used to
prevent glass surfaces from scratching and to provide adhesion
and bonding of other metal oxide finishes to glass and other
surfaces with similar characteristics.  
When a glass surface is treated at a temperature between its strain
and deforming temperatures with the vapor of an organic titanate
ester or chelate, the titanate pyrolyzes to form a (TiO2)X coating on
the glass surface.
APPLICATIONS
A thin (<100 nm) film of (TiO2)X is virtually transparent.  These films
are believed to be bound to the glass surface by Ti-O-Si bonds.
After a lubricant is applied, these bonds impart considerable
scratch resistance to the glass and consequently greatly reduce 
its fragility.
Tyzor® TPT is generally preferred for glass treatment, but chelates
such as Tyzor® AA-75, TE, and PITA also have been used.  They
can be applied undiluted, as a vapor,1-3 or in a solvent such as ethyl
acetate,4,5 kerosene,6 silicone fluid,7 or benzyl alcohol.8
The solvent application procedure is reported to provide a more

2  如何制造适用于超高黑度的特种炭黑？
原生粒径大小不同的炭黑混合物通常具有较低的黑度。
粒径分布宽或粒径分布窄但有较大颗粒形成长尾结构的
炭黑也是如此。我们创新部门经验丰富的技术人员勇于
迎接挑战，利用独特的中试反应器，将聚集体尺寸和原
生粒径尺寸变为几乎单峰分布曲线。 
小粒径的炉法炭黑适用于水性体系。我们的目标是研发
出一款既适用于溶剂型体系也适用于水性体系的炉法炭
黑。为达目标，我们采用了基于炉法的炭黑，并对其
进行特殊后处理。此后处理增加了含氧基团（如羧酸
基团）的数量，从而让表面具有更多的极性。因此，该
炭黑最终既适用于溶剂型体系，也适用于水性体系。这
样，用户仅需购买一种产品，即可满足两种不同的应用
需求。不仅降低了应用的复杂性，更重要的是，同一款
产品可应用在不同领域，如汽车原厂漆 (OEM) 和汽车修
补漆。

INTRODUCTION
Micro Powders manufactures micronized wax powders, many of which are based on polymers that have low surface 
energies. It is important to understand the proper techniques to use when incorporating micronized powders into 
a coating. To successfully “wet” the wax particles, the liquid used requires a lower surface tension than the surface 
energy of the wax. The term “wet” or “wetting” refers to completely dispersing individual micro-fine particles into a 
liquid medium.

GENERAL CONCEPT
It is generally recommended to avoid adding micronized wax directly into the final liquid formulation, as this typically 
will lead to poor wetting, insufficient dispersion, agglomerated wax particles, coating defects, and poor end use 
performance. It is usually difficult to wet out a small percentage of dry wax in a large batch of liquid material. Ideally, 
a concentrated dispersion is first prepared using the wax powder and one or more components of the coating 
formulation. The appropriate amount of this pre-dispersion is then added to the final coating.
UNDERSTANDING WAX DENSITY
The density of a wax additive affects how the wax behaves once dispersed into a liquid. If the density of the wax is 
lower than the density of the liquid, the wax will want to rise to the surface and float. If the density of the wax is higher 
than the density of the liquid, the wax will want to sink to t

If the wax dispersion process generates excessive foam, a defoamer can be added, typically at 0.5%.  Rhodoline® 643

General Formulation Guidance 
Recommended loading level for MicroTex 950 is  4.0 – 6.0% by total formula weight 
to achieve the optimal effect. Lower levels may reduce gloss and improve edge 
coverage but will not form a texture. Too high of a level may restrict the flow of the 
coating and result in a discontinuous film.
