DESCRIPTION
Dorf Ketal Tyzor® organic titanates and zirconates are used to
prevent glass surfaces from scratching and to provide adhesion
and bonding of other metal oxide finishes to glass and other
surfaces with similar characteristics.  
When a glass surface is treated at a temperature between its strain
and deforming temperatures with the vapor of an organic titanate
ester or chelate, the titanate pyrolyzes to form a (TiO2)X coating on
the glass surface.
APPLICATIONS
A thin (<100 nm) film of (TiO2)X is virtually transparent.  These films
are believed to be bound to the glass surface by Ti-O-Si bonds.
After a lubricant is applied, these bonds impart considerable
scratch resistance to the glass and consequently greatly reduce 
its fragility.

storage.  Because Tyzor® TPT does not expand while freezing,
containers will not bulge or show signs that the product has frozen.  
Tyzor® TPT is 100% tetraisopropyl titanate and mixing is not
necessary to obtain a homogeneous product when thawing.  
If frozen, Tyzor® TPT can be melted or thawed by careful, 
controlled heating.  Overheating beyond the recommended
temperature or prolonged storage at the limit of the recommended
thawing temperature can cause the product to yellow significantly.

DESCRIPTION
Dorf Ketal Tyzor® organic titanates are valuable catalysts in a
number of chemical reactions.  They are particularly useful in
esterifications and transesterifications, because the catalysis is
accomplished with a minimum of undesirable side reactions and
results in a high yield of esters with good color and odor.  
For example, esters can be made from carboxylic acids and
secondary alcohols with only a minor amount of olefin formation, 
in contrast to conventional acid catalysis, which produces
substantial amounts of olefins.

reactants and amino alcohols.  Similarly, titanates are condensation
catalysts in the manufacture of polyesters.  Organic titanates are

energy of the wax. The term “wet” or “wetting” refers to completely dispersing individual micro-fine particles into a 
liquid medium.
